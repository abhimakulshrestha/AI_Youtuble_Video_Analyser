YouTube AI Analyzer

A side panel for turning YouTube videos into useful notes, answers, watch plans, and study material with links back to the moments that matter.

Download tester build Installation steps

What it can do

Analyze at your depth

Switch between quick, deep, and visual analysis. Visual analysis checks a few sampled frames for information the narration may miss.

Watch with context

Create a goal-based watch plan, follow the current topic as the video plays, and ask questions about a specific moment.

Trace the evidence

Jump to supporting timestamps, see uncertainty labels, and check selected factual claims against outside sources.

Keep learning

Save insights in a local library, compare saved videos, and review quizzes and flashcards linked to their source clips.

How it works

  1. Open a YouTube video and click the extension icon to open its side panel.
  2. The extension reads available captions. Choose Quick Analyze or Deep Analyze to generate the first result.
  3. For Analyze visuals, keep the video tab open while the extension samples three frames. It restores your playback position afterward.
  4. Explore timestamped insights, ask questions, or save useful material. Saved items stay in this browser profile.

Analysis needs a transcript. Long videos may use evenly spaced excerpts, and visual analysis does not inspect every frame. AI-generated answers and outside claim checks can be incomplete, so use the linked evidence to verify important points.

Install the tester build

  1. Download the ZIP above and extract it to a folder. The extracted folder must contain manifest.json.
  2. In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder, not the ZIP file.
  4. Open or refresh a YouTube watch page, then click the YouTube AI Analyzer extension icon. Pin it from Chrome's Extensions menu for easier access.

This is a tester installation, not a Chrome Web Store listing. To update it, replace the extracted files with a new build, click Reload on chrome://extensions, and refresh your YouTube tab.

Data and availability

Video captions, selected screenshots, and questions are sent to the hosted analyzer when needed. Saved notes and videos stay in this browser profile; they do not sync across devices. The hosted API must be available for analysis to work.

YouTube AI Analyzer is an independent tool and is not affiliated with YouTube.
